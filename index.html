FAQ

Common questions.

How is my roadmap personalized?

It uses your field, level, region, goal, current tools and skills, proof of work and weekly time. Change any answer and the plan changes.

Who is it for?

Students, fresh graduates, working professionals, freelancers and career switchers.

Is it free?

Yes. The assessment and roadmap are free. A detailed report is optional.

Does it guarantee a job?

No. It gives direction and readiness insights. It does not guarantee employment or salary.
